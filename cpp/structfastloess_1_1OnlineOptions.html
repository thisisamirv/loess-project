Detailed Description

Options for online LOESS.

Identical fields to LoessOptions but has no parallel option: online LOESS processes one point at a time and always runs sequentially. Confidence/prediction intervals, standard errors, cross-validation, and diagnostics/residuals are Batch-only (or Batch/Streaming-only) and have no equivalent here.
